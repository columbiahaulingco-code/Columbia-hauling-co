Junk Removal FAQ

Full answers about pricing, pickup, items, scheduling, and disposal.

Text Photos for Quote
How much does junk removal cost?

Pricing depends on load size, weight, labor, access, and disposal costs. Text photos to 971-336-6379 for the fastest quote.

Can you give me a quote from photos?

Yes. Send a few clear photos, your city, where the junk is located, and when you want it picked up.

What items do you take?

We usually haul furniture, mattresses, appliances, yard debris, garage junk, rental cleanouts, and small construction debris.

What items do you not take?

Some items may not be accepted, including chemicals, paint, fuel, oil, asbestos, food waste, moldy items, and items with bugs.

Do you offer same-day pickup?

Sometimes. It depends on route availability, job size, dump hours, and how fast you approve the quote.

Do I need to move everything outside?

No. We can remove items from inside, garages, upstairs areas, and outside piles. Access can affect price.

Do you load everything?

Yes. We do the loading. You do not need to help.

What areas do you service?

We serve Vancouver WA, Portland OR, Camas, Washougal, Battle Ground, and nearby areas.

Do you recycle or donate usable items?

When possible, usable items may be donated or recycled instead of dumped.

Where does the junk go?

Junk goes to the proper disposal, recycling, or donation location depending on what it is.

Do you accept cash, card, Venmo, Zelle, or check?

Payment options can vary by job. Ask when you book and we will confirm.
